# Operate Sales and Service Cloud

Read [runtime contract](references/runtime-contract.md) before operating.

## Workflow

1. Identify the business lifecycle, record types, support/sales processes, statuses, assignment/escalation rules, queues, ownership and integrations.

2. Trace transitions and their writers, including conversion mappings, duplicate/matching rules, email-to-case, entitlement processes, milestones and scheduled actions where relevant.

3. For routing problems, inspect eligibility, presence, capacity, routing configuration and actual agent access before changing automation.

4. Check standard capabilities and current upstream specialist skills before adding custom Apex. Verify edition and feature availability.

5. Test realistic personas and status transitions, time-based behavior, duplicate requests, bulk entry and integration-created records.

6. Deliver process map, configuration/code changes, acceptance results and operational ownership. Route quoting to CPQ and external delivery to integration workflows.

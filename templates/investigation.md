# Investigation record

## Scope
Issue, business impact, environment, timestamp/timezone, actor and expected outcome.

## Evidence
| Observation | Source/location | Interpretation | Confidence | Missing check |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

## Cause assessment
Confirmed cause, supporting evidence, alternative hypotheses and remaining unknowns.

## Proposed change
Affected components, preserved behavior, risks, dependencies and recovery.

## Verification
Actual commands/results, environment, test coverage scope and unexecuted checks.

## Stakeholder update
What failed, impact, resolution status and decisions required in plain language.
